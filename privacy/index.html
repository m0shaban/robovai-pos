Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how RoboVAI POS collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use our desktop software, mobile WMS tools, and website at https://pos.robovai.tech.

1. Data Privacy & Local Storage

RoboVAI POS is architected as an offline-first solution. Your sales transactions, customer databases, and product inventories are stored locally on your own machine. We do not sell or monetize your business data.

2. Third-Party Platform Authentication

When connecting third-party platforms (such as TikTok, Telegram, or Meta), authentication tokens are securely encrypted and retained solely to perform requested automation actions (such as posting authorized promotional video content or sending daily sales alerts).

3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard cryptographic practices to protect all credentials and sensitive operational communications.

4. User Rights and Data Deletion

You may disconnect any third-party app integration or request deletion of stored automation credentials at any time by contacting our support team or clearing application settings.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: privacy@robovai.tech or visit pos.robovai.tech.
